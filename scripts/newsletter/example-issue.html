Why applications get rejected

1 min read

You were rejected before anyone reached your second page

Your CV was open for eleven seconds. The reader was not weighing your career. They were looking for one thing the posting asked for, and it was not in the top half of your first page.

They did not decide you could not do the job. They decided they could not tell.

Nobody will write and tell you which line lost it. Next week the same page goes out again.
Hiring trends

1 min read

Postings stopped asking for machine learning and started naming the stack

Descriptions now name the framework, the warehouse and the orchestration layer.

That is not a wish list. A team naming its stack is describing a system that already exists, and telling you which evidence it will recognise.
