Your CV was open for eleven seconds. The reader was not weighing your career. They were looking for one thing the posting asked for, and it was not in the top half of your first page.

They did not decide you could not do the job. They decided they could not tell.

Nobody will write and tell you which line lost it. Next week the same page goes out again.
