# When to contact a recruiter after applying

Contact the recruiter when you can add useful context, not simply ask whether they saw your CV.

Mention the role, state one relevant reason you may be a strong fit and ask a simple question. Keep the message short.

Example: I applied for the project coordinator role. My experience managing cross border projects closely matches the role's stakeholder focus. Would it be useful if I shared a short example?
